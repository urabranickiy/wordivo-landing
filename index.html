The science

Built on 140 years of memory research

Spaced repetition isn't a gimmick. It's the most studied learning technique in cognitive psychology — and Wordivo applies it automatically to every word you learn.

We lose around 70% of new information within a day. Unless something brings it back — just before it goes.

In 1885, Hermann Ebbinghaus discovered the forgetting curve. The fix has been known since: review at increasing intervals, each one timed just before you'd forget.

Wordivo's 10-stage algorithm applies the same memory science used by flashcard methods trusted by millions of medical students and language learners worldwide.

  1. 01Ebbinghaus, 1885 — the forgetting curve, measured on himself
  2. 02Spaced intervals — each review lands later than the last
  3. 03Active recall — pulling a word from memory beats re-reading it

Practice variety

Many ways to practice the same word

Variety prevents boredom and forces the brain to retrieve information from different angles — recognition, recall, context, production. That's how words stop being "studied" and start being known.
