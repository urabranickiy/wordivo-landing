2. What we collect

  • Telegram identifiers: your Telegram user ID, username, first name, and language setting (provided by Telegram when you use the bot).
  • Learning data: words and translations you add, practice answers, progress, schedules, and activity timestamps.
  • Teacher/student relationships: group membership and assignments.
  • Payment data: processed by LiqPay (PrivatBank), our payment service provider. We receive transaction status and limited billing metadata — we never see or store your full card details.

3. How we use it

  • To provide and personalize practice (scheduling, exercises, progress tracking).
  • To operate teacher–student features and payouts.
  • To process subscriptions (via LiqPay) and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate service and billing information.

Legal bases (GDPR): performance of contract, legitimate interests, and consent where required.

4. Who we share it with (sub-processors)

  • Telegram — message delivery (the platform the bot runs on).
  • LiqPay (PrivatBank) — payment processing. Wordivo is the seller of record and is responsible for the underlying sale, not LiqPay.
  • Microsoft — text-to-speech. To pronounce a word or read a sentence aloud, the text is sent to Microsoft's speech service. That text is your own vocabulary, so please don't save anything private as a "word". Audio is cached so the same text is sent once, not on every review.
  • Hosting/infrastructure: Railway (application), Neon (database) and Vercel (this website).
  • Teacher payouts are transferred manually by us to the bank/payment details the teacher provides in-app; we don't route payouts through a fixed third-party payout provider.

We do not sell your personal data.

This website loads no third-party fonts, scripts, analytics or cookies — everything it needs is served from our own domain, so visiting it does not tell anyone else that you did.

5. International transfers

Your data may be processed outside Ukraine/EU by the providers above, under appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses where required).

6. Retention

Learning data is kept while your account exists, and is deleted within 30 days of an erasure request or after 24 months of complete inactivity. Payment records are kept for 3 years to meet accounting obligations — when an account is erased those records stay, but the link to you is removed, so what remains is an amount and a date with no person attached.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, export, or restriction of your data, and you may object to certain processing. To exercise any right, email hello@wordivo.co. EU users may also complain to their local data-protection authority.

8. Children

Wordivo is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided data without consent, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in-app or by email.
