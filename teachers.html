How it works

Three steps. Thirty seconds.

After each lesson, add words. The algorithm handles everything else.

1

Add words with translations

Type words in one message. Assign to individual students or entire groups. You control every translation.

2

Algorithm schedules reviews

Based on the forgetting curve. Each word resurfaces at the perfect interval — just before your student would forget.

3

You see it land

Your student list shows how many words each one has mastered — and their voice notes using your words arrive in your DMs.
